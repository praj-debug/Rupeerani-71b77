About RupeeRani

Knowledge for money, work and business.

RupeeRani is an education-first ecosystem helping people make sense of personal finance, careers and the businesses shaping everyday life.

Our story

Built from questions real people face.

RupeeRani grew from the belief that important topics like money, credit, careers and business should not feel intimidating or reserved for insiders. Founded by Prajakta Mane, a FinTech professional with experience across lending, financial products, customer journeys and operations, it brings practical industry perspective into clear, approachable learning.

The goal is not to tell people what to think or promise quick wins. It is to help them ask better questions, understand their options and feel more confident making their own decisions.

Mission

Make knowledge usable.

Turn complex financial, career and business topics into clear, practical education people can apply in real life.

Vision

More informed decisions.

Build a trusted learning ecosystem where the next generation can navigate money, work and business with greater clarity and confidence.

What to expect

Learn without the noise.

Beginner-friendly explainers, practical tools, career resources, business breakdowns and honest conversations grounded in evidence, not hype.

Our scope

Three connected areas. One clear purpose.

Personal finance and investing education, career and professional resources, and accessible explanations of companies, financial products and business trends.
